### Recommendations
- **Ship the reason_tag event schema now on the scripted prototype, pull GOROD-055 to P0** _(low/high)_ — The reason_tag corpus is the only irreproducible moat (brief 05, architecture doc), uncopyable retroactively because Spotify and Yandex have no collection interface. The prototype proves the mechanic without capturing output: TwinrWhy GOROD-041 at gorod-fm.html L13235 writes only a facet-id array to localStorage gorodfm_rejected and bumps the wave, so every reject, like, skip, dial and text-steer is a labeled preference pair thrown away. Buildable today on scripted data; session_vector is a stub now, real later, but the schema and collection habit start immediately. Low effort, ROI is the entire defensibility thesis plus the most investor-legible artifact.
- **Define a Reason Provenance Contract enforced by an offline fidelity audit plus online NLI gate** _(medium/high)_ — The north star explanation-equals-real-vector is unfalsifiable today because nothing binds the displayed reason to the retrieval cause. Brief 04 and AUDIT section 4 name this the Spotify DJ Hacker News failure: narrate beautifully but unbound to the vector, verify once, trust gone forever. Specify: each track returns a typed provenance object with cause cf_neighbour or clap_attribute or steer_op or context plus evidence and contribution_weight; why-strings templated from provenance, closed-world, never free-generated (the prototype REASONS array at L13206 is the right shape but must become server-derived); a CI fidelity audit asserts the stated cause is in the top-k actual scoring contributions and tracks a fidelity-violation-rate; an NLI gate using DeBERTa runs only on the narrative layer, cite-or-suppress. Tie to the reject mechanic so a rejected reason drops its contribution_weight on the next pick.
- **Unify steering on one session-intent vector behind a typed SteerOp router with a durable Twinr confirmation gate** _(medium/high)_ — Brief 02 sections 4 and 7 require an ephemeral session-intent vector that every steer mutates versus a durable Twinr profile updated via slow EMA plus confirmation gate, with CTRL-Rec compiling NL into a typed SteerOp as one embedding op of about 10 to 50 ms, never a generative LLM call on the hot path. The prototype four steer surfaces (TwinrWhy reject L13235, WaveDials L13192, per-track, text-chat) are decoupled scripts that independently bump the wave with no shared intent object. Introduce a single SessionIntent module that all four call with a typed op of type hard_filter or soft_rerank or embedding_nudge or diversity_boost or constraint plus payload and source_surface. Gives the transition ramp its substrate (apply over two to three tracks via a novelty floor), feeds reason_tag the session_vector_snapshot, makes the confirmation gate concrete. RU-first router is a correctness requirement: test sdelai temnee, menshe rep, chto-to pod dorogu.
- **Sequence the recsys so each piece earns its data: Stage-0 CLAP-only, Stage-1 Word2Vec-CF plus epsilon-greedy, Stage-2 SASRec plus Thompson, never start neural** _(high/high)_ — Brief 01 section 8: do not start neural, you will not have the data; ALS or Word2Vec-CF plus musicnn plus epsilon-greedy ships and is debuggable. Stage-0 for demo or CC catalog uses an item store of CLAP plus metadata only, since CLAP carries cold-start, the why via text-queryable audio, and steering as the same vector op; retrieval is pgvector ANN to about 10M vectors, selection epsilon-greedy, why a CLAP-attribute match that is honest because it is a real vector distance. Stage-1 on a MERLIN or Yambda seed adds a CF tower via Word2Vec-on-sessions aligned into CLAP space via contrastive init CLCRec, bootstrapped on free Yambda-5B. Stage-2 post-PMF adds a SASRec re-ranker on graded decaying reward (skip is not dislike), graduates epsilon-greedy to Thompson, adds BaRT with reason as a bandit arm. Validate the CLAP checkpoint license and GPU cost before committing it as the spine; bake an explore floor into candidate gen from Stage-0 or the flow collapses to hits.
- **Draw the headless-backend versus client line explicitly and precompute next-track plus provenance during the current track** _(medium/medium)_ — Latency budget brief 02 section 5: under 800 ms steer-to-next-track, under 2 s prompt-to-playlist. Headless holds the embedding pipeline, ANN index pgvector to Qdrant, GBDT or SASRec ranker, BaRT bandit, reason_tag append-only store, durable Twinr EMA store, SteerOp CTRL-Rec embedding op, Provenance Contract and fidelity audit, LLM gateway with small versus large routing and semantic cache for async DJ commentary. Precompute next track plus provenance during the current track (doc: trivially under 100 ms; transition card GOROD-048 is the perceptual cover). Forbid generative LLM on the per-skip or per-steer path. Moat assets stay server-side feeding the Twinr ad cabinet as taste_segment, first-party, no PII.
